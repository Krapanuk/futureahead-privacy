FutureAhead — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 June 2026

FutureAhead does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • No account or sign-up is required.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no third-party SDKs.
  • The settings the app stores — the target date you choose and your optional wake / bedtime — are saved on your device (in an App Group container) and shared only with the app's own Home Screen / Lock Screen widgets and — if you use an Apple Watch — with the FutureAhead watch complication via Apple's on-device WatchConnectivity.
  • Apple Health: if you grant permission, the app reads your recent sleep data from Apple Health solely to suggest your wake and bedtime. This reading happens entirely on your device; the data is only used to fill in those two times and is never stored separately, sent to any server, or shared. You can decline this access and set the times manually instead.
  • None of this data ever leaves your devices and is never sent to any server.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to request, correct, or delete beyond removing the app.
