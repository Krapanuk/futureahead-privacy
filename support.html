FutureAhead — Support

Help with the FutureAhead countdown app.

FutureAhead counts down the years, months and days to a date you choose, with a live countdown on your iPhone, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and Apple Watch.

Contact

Questions, feedback or a problem? Email oliverbeine@gmx.net and we'll get back to you.

Quick help

  • Set your date: tap the date on the main screen and pick your target day.
  • Add a widget: long-press the Home or Lock Screen, tap +, search for FutureAhead.
  • Apple Watch: install via the Watch app on your iPhone, then long-press your watch face → Edit → Complications → FutureAhead.
  • Waking-hours countdown: set your wake and bedtime in the app, or let it suggest them from Apple Health (optional, read on-device only).

Privacy

FutureAhead collects no data. See the Privacy Policy.
